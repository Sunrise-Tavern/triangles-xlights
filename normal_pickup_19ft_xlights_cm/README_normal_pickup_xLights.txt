NORMAL FULL-SIZE PICKUP REFERENCE MODEL
=======================================

Files
-----
normal_pickup_19ft_cm.obj
normal_pickup_19ft_materials.mtl

Units and axes
--------------
Units: centimeters
X: vehicle length, positive toward front
Y: vehicle width
Z: height
Origin: center of overall footprint at ground level

Final envelope
--------------
Length: 579.12 cm (19.00 ft)
Width:  203.20 cm (80.0 in)
Height: 198.12 cm (78.0 in)

Geometry represented
--------------------
- Generic full-size crew-cab pickup
- Open bed
- Hood, cab, windows, doors, mirrors
- Four wheels, bumpers, and basic lights

Limitations
-----------
This is a simplified scene-reference model for xLights, not a specific
make/model or an engineering-accurate vehicle model.

xLights
-------
Keep the OBJ and MTL files together. Import the OBJ as a static mesh and
preserve equal X, Y, and Z scale values.
