M989A1 HEMAT EMPTY TRAILER REFERENCE MODEL
==========================================

Files

Units and axes
--------------
Units: centimeters
X: trailer length, positive toward towbar
Y: trailer width
Z: height
Origin: center of published overall envelope at ground level

Nominal dimensions
------------------
Overall length: 784.86 cm (309 in)
Overall width:  249.94 cm (98.4 in)
Overall height: 144.78 cm (57 in)
Cargo deck:    447.04 x 243.84 cm (176 x 96 in)

Geometry represented
--------------------
- Four-wheel lowboy trailer
- Central wood cargo deck
- Raised fore and aft tread-plate decks
- Removable side/end panel silhouette
- Towbar, lunette, safety-chain silhouette
- Front spare tire and rear storage box

Limitations
-----------
This is a deliberately simplified visual reference for xLights, not an
engineering model. Suspension, steering linkage, fasteners, tie-down rings,
exact panel framing, and underbody details are approximate.
