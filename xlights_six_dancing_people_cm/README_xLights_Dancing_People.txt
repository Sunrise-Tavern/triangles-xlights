SIX LOW-POLY DANCING PEOPLE FOR xLIGHTS
========================================

Included poses
--------------
1. Disco point
2. Both hands up
3. Side groove
4. Spin with arms extended
5. One-leg kick
6. Low bounce / crouched dance pose

Units and axes
--------------
Units: centimeters
X: left/right
Y: forward/back
Z: height
Origin: approximately centered between the feet at ground level

Import notes
------------
Each dancer is a separate OBJ model with its own matching MTL file.
Keep each OBJ beside its corresponding MTL file. Import each OBJ as a
static 3D mesh/object in xLights.

Use equal X, Y, and Z scale values. These are intentionally simplified,
low-poly scene-reference figures rather than realistic human scans.
